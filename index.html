About

Get in Touch

I design digital products, but I started in architecture and interior design — drawn to how spaces shape the way people feel and behave. Now I apply that same spatial thinking to software: where does the friction live, and what's the smallest change that removes it?

At DePaul University, I pair fast product decisions with research rigor — designing tools people actually adopt, not just tools that test well. Every project below shipped, was pitched, or was tested with the people it's for.
